**Dicta now has a referral program.** Share your personal link with a friend. When they sign up and pay for their first subscription, you both get free minutes: currently **60 for you and 60 for your friend**.

The minutes are not a coupon with an expiry date. They go straight into your balance and are spent on recording, transcription and AI summaries, just like the minutes in your plan.

## How it works in three steps

1. **Share your link.** Open Dicta, tap **Invite friends** and send the message to Telegram, WhatsApp or anywhere else with one tap. You can also copy the link or show a QR code in person.
2. **Your friend signs up.** They open the link, create an account on the web or in the app and, if they install the app first, enter your code at sign-up.
3. **Your friend subscribes.** As soon as their first subscription payment goes through, both of you get 60 minutes.

Right now the bonus is 60 minutes for each side. It is set by Dicta and can change, so check the amount on the invite screen before you share.

## Who qualifies

- **The friend must be new to Dicta.** The invite counts for accounts created through your link or code. If your friend forgot to use it, they can still enter your code in the first days after signing up (currently 7), as long as they have not paid for anything yet.
- **The first subscription is what counts.** The reward is for a friend's first paid subscription. Minute packs and later renewals do not trigger it.
- **You cannot invite yourself.** Your own account does not count, and neither does a payment made in a workspace you are a member of.
- **There is a time limit and a cap.** The subscription has to happen within a set period after sign-up (currently 90 days), and one person can earn rewards for a limited number of friends (currently 50).

## When the minutes arrive and where to see them

The minutes arrive **right after your friend's payment succeeds**. You both get a notification in the app. You can see the bonus on the usage screen under **Extra minutes**.

Extra minutes are spent after the monthly minutes of your plan are used up, and they do not expire at the end of the month. Your friend's minutes go to the workspace that paid, and yours go to your personal workspace.

## What if the payment is refunded

If your friend's qualifying payment is refunded, the bonus is reversed: the minutes from that invite are taken back from both of you. Your balance never drops below zero, and your other invites are not affected.

## Where to find your link

- **In the mobile app:** open **Settings** and tap **Invite friends**.
- **On the web:** use **Invite friends** in the sidebar. The page also lists your friends and shows who has subscribed and who is still pending.

Your code is permanent, so a link you sent a month ago still works. The page shows only a friend's first name and last initial, never their email.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many free minutes do I get for inviting a friend?**
Currently 60 minutes for you and 60 minutes for your friend. The amount is set by Dicta and can change, but the minutes you have already received stay yours.

**When do the minutes arrive?**
Right after your friend's first subscription payment goes through. You both get a notification, and the minutes appear in your balance as Extra minutes.

**Does my friend have to be a new Dicta user?**
Yes. The invite works for people who sign up with your link or enter your code in their first days after creating an account, before they have paid for anything.

**What happens if my friend's payment is refunded?**
The bonus is reversed for both of you: the minutes from that invite are taken back, and your balance is never reduced below zero.

**Where do I find my invite link?**
In the Dicta app open Settings and tap Invite friends. On the web it is in the sidebar under Invite friends.
