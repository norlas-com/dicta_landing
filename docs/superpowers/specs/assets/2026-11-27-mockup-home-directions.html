"The page is a meeting": three ways to show it

Each is the first screen of dicta.uz with its own Dicta look (paper, indigo, Literata). The animation is the idea: your visit is being recorded and turned into notes. Copy is English here; the real page stays uz/ru/en. Click the one that feels most like us. You can also say "A's margin with C's ending", for example.

A

Margin transcript

A normal long page with an agenda on the side. On the left, a transcript of your visit fills in as you scroll, with timestamps and speakers ("You", "Dicta"). Each section is an agenda item. At the end, the margin turns into the recap of your visit.

B

Live captions

The page "speaks" as you scroll: each section's text lights up word by word, like Dicta's live transcription. A dark caption bar at the bottom shows what's being heard, with a waveform and a timer. The last section is the recap.

C

Meeting room + notes

The screen is split. On the left, a stylised call: you and Dicta, a timer, a waveform. On the right, the notes write themselves as you scroll: summary, then decisions, then tasks. At the end, the right side is a finished Dicta recap, and the button is the task "Sign up".
